Privacy Policy

Destination Alarm · Effective 12 November 2026

Destination Alarm wakes you before you reach your bus, train or metro stop. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it. In short: your data stays on your phone. We have no server, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your data.

Data the app uses

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Precise and approximate locationTo track your journey and ring the alarm before your stop. Used only while a journey is active.On your phone only. The current journey's route is deleted when the journey ends.
Name, email and passwordTo create your account and log in.On your phone only. The password is stored as a salted hash, never as text.
Journey history and saved placesTo show your recent journeys and let you reuse places.On your phone only.
App settingsAlarm sound, snooze length, theme and similar choices.On your phone only.

The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Services the app contacts

To work, the app sends limited data directly from your phone to these services. Each has its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Your phone's built-in geocoder (usually Google)The place name you search for, or a point's coordinatesWhen you search for a destination or share a location
OpenStreetMap (Nominatim search and map tiles)The place name you search for, or a point's coordinates; the map area you viewWhen the phone's geocoder finds nothing, and when a map is shown
Google MapsA Google Maps link you share into the appOnly when you share a Google Maps link, to read the place from it
Ads feed (our website host)A standard web request (your IP address); no personal dataAbout twice a day, to show the Offers & Updates section

Like any web request, these services can see your phone's IP address. The app never sends your name, email, password or journey history to anyone.

Ads

The Home screen shows promotions chosen by the app's administrator. They are plain cards loaded from a file; they do not track you, and tapping one simply opens a web page in your browser.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, journey history and saved places from the phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing else for us to delete. See Delete your account.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
