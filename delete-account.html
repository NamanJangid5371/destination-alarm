Delete your Destination Alarm account

Destination Alarm · How to delete your account and data

Your Destination Alarm account exists only on your phone. We have no server and keep no copy of your account, so you can delete it yourself at any time, instantly.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Destination Alarm.
  2. If a journey is running, end it first.
  3. Go to Settings (bottom bar) → Account → Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Or uninstall the app

Uninstalling Destination Alarm deletes all of its data from the phone, including your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email and password
  • Your journey history
  • Your saved places

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent to us. App settings (such as alarm sound) stay on the phone until you uninstall the app.
